The one thing we need from you: A or B

A says the screen should be exciting. Points, a daily streak, and a weekly ranking of the branch by name. It has the highest chance of getting people to open it, and it puts the bottom half of the branch on a public list every week, which is exactly the people who need the training most.

B says it should be useful. Your open loans pick what you read, and your closed files show what keeps going wrong. Nothing is public. It is cheaper to build, but about forty topics have to be written before it means anything, and that is Andrew's job rather than Jawad's.

We recommend B. If a loan officer asks why they should spend twenty minutes on this instead of on a call, A answers "to move up the board" and B answers "because Delgado is Thursday and you have never done one of these." The second answer survives a bad week.

Every screen has a switch marked Show the plumbing. Off, it reads in plain English. On, every number shows where it would come from and what would have to be built.
