What we need from you

A or B. Both use the same lessons, the same guides and the same app shell, so the difference is only what the screen is for.

AThe screen should be excitingopen ↗

Finishing a lesson pays points and moves your row up the branch board the app already has. The highest chance of getting anyone to open it.

Against: it publishes a ranked list of seventeen people by name every week, and the bottom half is exactly who needs the training. It is also the higher of the two to build.

BThe screen should be usefulopen ↗

Your live pipeline picks what you read, and your closed files show what keeps going wrong. Nothing is public and nothing is scored.

Against: about forty topics have to be written before it means anything, and that work is Andrew's rather than Jawad's. It is also the quieter of the two, so it gives no launch moment.

we recommend

B, and keep A's points as a private counter if something visible is wanted. If a loan officer asks why they should spend twenty minutes here instead of on a call, A answers "to move up the board" and B answers "because Delgado is Thursday and you have never done one of these." The second answer survives a bad week.
