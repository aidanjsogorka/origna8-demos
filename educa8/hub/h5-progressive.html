The one thing we need: A or B

A says the screen should be exciting, B says it should be useful. We recommend B. If a loan officer asks why they should spend twenty minutes here instead of on a call, A answers "to move up the board" and B answers "because Delgado is Thursday and you have never done one of these." The second answer survives a bad week. Everything below opens in place if you want the detail.

Both versions ask you a question a few days after you read something, because that is when people forget. It is the strongest thing in either version and it is not a game trick, it is just how memory works. We could do both eventually, but not in the first build: whichever arrives second gets read as decoration on the first.

Two things are needed by every version and so are not counted as a difference: a record of who has read what, and a record of who answered which question. Anything Educa8 does needs both. Turning the switch on inside any screen lists every number on it and where it would come from.
