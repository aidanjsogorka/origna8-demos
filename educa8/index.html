Educa8, rebuilt

The learning section, rebuilt in two halves: the screen Andrew writes on and the screen loan officers read on. The reading screen is built twice, because there are two good answers to why anybody would open it. Picking one is a business call, not a design one.

We recommend B. Asked why they should spend twenty minutes here instead of on a call, A answers "to move up the board" and B answers "because Delgado is Thursday and you have never done one of these." The second answer survives a bad week.

The same lessons built five times, so the look could be picked by comparison instead of argument. 05 won and became the three screens at the top. The other four are kept because the reasons they lost are worth knowing, and all five carry the plumbing switch.

What is fixed
Sidebar, top bar, tokens, Hanken Grotesk. Every screen sits inside the real product, not a microsite.
What is wrong today
No reason to start · progress that means nothing · six identical cards · the branch scoreboard sitting unused.
Who you are looking at
A loan officer partway through, never an empty account. In A, 3 of 29 lessons done. In B, 12 open loans.
Known gap
The app has a light theme behind the sun and moon toggle. These are dark only.

On colour. Everything uses Origna8's real colours, sampled from what the app puts on screen rather than the names in its stylesheet, which disagreed with each other. The logo runs orange to pink to purple to blue, warmer and younger than anything the product uses elsewhere. Orange means one thing here: this is yours, this is next.

Every screen is fully interactive and carries the plumbing switch. The bar along the bottom jumps between all nine.
