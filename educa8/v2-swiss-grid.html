Chapter 01

What Is a Mortgage?

A mortgage is a loan used to purchase real estate, where the property itself serves as collateral.

That single sentence carries the whole structure. The borrower gets money now. The lender gets a claim on the property until the money comes back. Everything else in this course, qualifying ratios, disclosure timing, lock strategy, is a consequence of that trade.

Key concepts04 terms
Principal
The amount borrowed.
Interest
The cost of borrowing.
Amortization
How the loan is paid off over time.
Escrow
Funds held for taxes and insurance.

Read the four terms as a set, not a list. Principal and interest are what the borrower owes the lender. Amortization is the schedule that retires them. Escrow sits outside all three: it is money the borrower pays alongside the loan, held on their behalf, for obligations owed to somebody else entirely.
