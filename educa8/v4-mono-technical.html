A mortgage is a loan used to purchase real estate, where the property itself serves as collateral.

That single sentence carries the whole product. The lender is not betting on the borrower alone, it is betting on the borrower plus an asset it can take back. Everything downstream, the ratios, the disclosures, the lock, is a consequence of that arrangement.
